## Selected Case Studies

1. **Case Study 2 — Progressive Electricity Billing**  
   Calculates household electricity bills using progressive consumption slabs, fixed charges, surcharges, and rebates, and flags unusual monthly increases.

2. **Case Study 12 — Attendance Recovery Computation**  
   Calculates current attendance and the minimum number of additional fully attended classes needed to reach the 75% eligibility threshold.

3. **Case Study 22 — Laboratory Chemical Inventory Validation**  
   Validates a hypothetical classroom inventory against quantity limits and supplied storage-category compatibility rules.

4. **Case Study 34 — Student Feedback Sentiment Scoring**  
   Scores feedback using predefined positive and negative keywords and classifies each result as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

5. **Case Study 42 — Student Marks Matrix Analysis**  
   Calculates student and subject averages, identifies the top performer, and flags subjects with low average marks.

6. **Case Study 51 — Bus Passenger Occupancy Simulation**  
   Tracks passenger boarding and alighting at stops and checks occupancy against bus capacity.

7. **Case Study 64 — Tuple-Based Course Registration Validation**  
   Detects duplicate student-course registrations and course prerequisite violations using tuples and sets.

8. **Case Study 67 — Retail Order Dispatch and Return Analysis**  
   Validates order records and calculates outstanding quantities and net accepted deliveries.

9. **Case Study 86 — Multi-Stage ATM Transaction Audit**  
   Simulates deposits, withdrawals, and transfers; updates the balance; rejects invalid operations; and flags repeated high-value withdrawals.

10. **Case Study 92 — Multi-Event Sports Scheduling and Scoring**  
    Detects overlapping events at the same venue, aggregates team points, and generates final rankings.
